Boards built for the US/EU

Pay in the wrong units

"$85k a year" means little until it becomes "about Rp 117 million a month".

What makes it feel live

Voice and screen move together.

Cards before speech

Tools run in the browser, so the job cards appear as the agent says "let me check", before it reads the results.

The card follows the voice

When the agent says "number two" or a company name, that card lights up in time with the audio.

Tap or talk, one context

Ask about this on a card and typed messages go into the same conversation (conversation.message + reply.create).

Rupiah, not just dollars

Every job shows a monthly IDR estimate, and the agent says it: "about 131 million rupiah a month".

Honest eligibility

Open to Indonesia, not stated, or region-locked. "LATAM applicants only" is flagged and ranked lower.

No links read aloud

URLs are removed before the model sees results, so it cannot make up a link. Apply links come from the API unchanged.

Roadmap

From job search to getting hired.

  • Native Bahasa Indonesia speech in and out, once AssemblyAI adds Indonesian (today: mixed-language input understood, simple-English replies).
  • Phone line through AssemblyAI SIP + Twilio, for workers without a laptop.
  • Interview practice mode: the same agent runs a mock screening call for the job you picked.
  • Saved shortlists and "call me when a match appears" alerts.
  • Full job details behind LokerDollar's keyed API tier.
  • Employer side: a voice pre-screen that sends shortlisted candidates to employers.
